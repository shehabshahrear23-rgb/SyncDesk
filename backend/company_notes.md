SyncDesk company notes
======================
Everything you write BELOW the dashed line is given to the AI assistant,
so it can answer questions about your company.

Write in plain sentences. Ideas for what to add:
  - what the company does and who its customers are
  - office hours, holidays and leave rules
  - products, prices, policies, contact details

Save the file. The AI uses the new text on the very next question.
Do not put passwords or other secrets here.
